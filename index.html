The concept

A coach for the other 99%

Attune is a training and nutrition coaching app for amateur and recreational endurance athletes. It builds your training plan around your goal event, level and target time. Then it builds a nutrition plan from that same training.

The two stay connected. When your training changes, your nutrition changes with it automatically. Attune sits between cheap AI trackers and elite coaching that most of us can't justify.

>90%

of athletes worldwide are amateur or recreational.

Yet no product plans their training and nutrition together, let alone connects the two.

The problem

Most athletes are guessing how to fuel

As many as 1 in 5 athletes may be under-fuelling or fuelling poorly. That's a leading risk factor for Relative Energy Deficiency in Sport (RED-S). The signs are easy to mistake for overtraining, stress or a busy life: constant fatigue, frequent illness, poor sleep, slow recovery, and plateauing despite consistent effort.

The solution

One app for your training and your fuelling

Attune builds your training and your nutrition together and keeps adjusting both. You don't need to link a separate training platform.

01

AI training plan

Built around your goal event, current level and target time. Every session has a clear structure and a target pace, not just a distance.

02

Adaptive nutrition plan

Personalised from your training, goals and food preferences. If you miss a session, run faster or slower than planned, or hit a peak week, your targets adjust automatically.

03

A matched professional

An optional 1:1 add-on with a real sports coach, nutritionist or dietitian. You work with the same person over time, not a rotating pool or a chatbot.

How it works

  1. 1

    Plan generated on sign-up

    Built instantly from your goal event, level, target time and food preferences, with training and nutrition built together.

  2. 2

    Every session has real structure

    Long runs, intervals, tempo and brick sessions are split into warm-up, main set, recovery and cool-down, each with its own target pace.

  3. 3

    Adjusts automatically

    A peak week looks different from a recovery week. A missed or off-pace session changes both your future training and that day's nutrition.

  4. 4

    Matched with one professional

    If you add coaching, you keep the same coach, nutritionist or dietitian over time.

  5. 5

    Flexible logging

    Log meals manually, snap a photo, or pick from a recipe bank matched to your macros for the day.
